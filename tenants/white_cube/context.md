# White Cube

## What the company does

White Cube is a subscription app for visual artists. An artist uses it to keep an archive of images and files of their work, tag and search that archive, record which pieces showed in which exhibition, log sales, and share portfolio links. It is freemium: anyone can sign up for a free plan, and the paid plans unlock the working features. Users are mostly on mobile and spread across a dozen countries, with the United States about a third. History starts in June 2024, in US dollars.

## How the money flows

Visitors arrive from organic search, paid social, paid search, display, direct, referral, and email. A visitor signs up, and a little under half of signups start a seven-day trial. A trial that converts becomes a paid subscription on the Studio plan (12 dollars a month or 120 a year) or the Professional plan (29 a month or 290 a year); about a quarter of paid subscriptions are annual. Some free users subscribe without a trial, and some churned users come back. Subscribers upgrade, downgrade, and cancel. Invoices are issued per billing period and charged to a card, and a few percent of charges fail.

## Metrics in plain words

MRR is the sum of the monthly-equivalent price of every active subscription as of each day; an annual plan counts as its price divided by twelve. Active subscriptions is the count behind it. Both are levels, not flows: the MRR for a month is the value on its last day, and adding daily values together is meaningless. ARPU is MRR divided by active subscriptions.

New, expansion, contraction, and churned MRR are movements, dated by the day the event happened. New MRR includes reactivations and direct conversions. Net new MRR is new plus expansion minus contraction minus churned, and over any window it equals the change in MRR.

Revenue is cash: successful payment amounts by payment date. It differs from MRR because annual plans pay up front and because failed payments and retries shift timing. Payment failure rate is failed attempts over all attempts.

Trials started, trial conversions, and trial conversion rate are dated by the trial start day. Activated trials are trials whose user uploaded work during the trial. DAU is distinct active users per day and WAU is distinct users active in the trailing seven days. Member activity rate is the share of paying subscriber days with product activity.

## Gotchas

Trial conversion rate for the most recent week is incomplete, because those trials are still running. Customer churn rate divides churn events by a daily snapshot, so read it at day grain or treat a monthly figure as a rate per day. Plan on an MRR movement is the plan after the event; plan on a product event is the plan at the time of the event.

## What the data cannot answer

There is no cost data beyond ad spend, so no margin or profit. Feature-level usage is not exposed, nor are cohort retention curves, lifetime value, refunds, or taxes. Why a number moved is not something this data settles on its own.
